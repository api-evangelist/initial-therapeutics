# Initial Therapeutics

> Initial Therapeutics, Inc. is a preclinical-stage biotechnology company in South San Francisco,
> California. It discovers and develops small-molecule medicines designed to halt pathogenic
> protein formation at its earliest stage, using a proprietary STOPS platform (Selective
> Termination of Protein Synthesis) that intercepts translation of a target protein inside the
> ribosome exit tunnel. It is a drug discovery company, not a software vendor.
